The Planner application ("we", "our", or "the app") values the privacy of its users and is committed to protecting any personal information entered while using the app. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected, in accordance with Google Play guidelines and applicable data protection best practices.

By using Planner, you agree to the terms described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information Collected

Planner collects only the personal information voluntarily provided by the user that is strictly necessary for the app’s core functionality. This information includes: name, phone number, email address, and task data (such as title, date, time, and completion status).

All information is stored exclusively on the user’s device. The app does not perform any automatic data collection, does not use remote servers, and does not share data with any third parties.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

The stored information is solely used to enable the main features of the app, such as creating, editing, and deleting tasks, organizing them by date and time, and marking their completion status. None of the collected data is used for marketing, analytics, or advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored locally within the device’s internal database. No information is transmitted to external servers, APIs, or third-party services. The app follows good software development practices to ensure data integrity and security. However, we cannot be held responsible for issues resulting from factors beyond the app’s control, such as device loss or unauthorized access caused by malware.

4. Data Sharing

Planner does not share, sell, rent, or transfer any personal information to third parties. No information is used for advertising or external communication of any kind.

5. App Permissions

The app does not request or require any special system permissions. Planner does not access the camera, microphone, location, contacts, external storage, or any other sensitive resources.

6. User Rights

Users have full control over the information they provide in the app. You can edit or delete your data and tasks at any time. Uninstalling the app will permanently remove all data stored locally on the device.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

The information remains stored on the user’s device only while the app is installed. Once the app is uninstalled, all data is permanently and irreversibly deleted.
